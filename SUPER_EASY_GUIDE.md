## ✨ What the AI Does For You

When you click "Generate Smart Data with AI", the system:

### 🎯 Analyzes Your Project
- Reads your deal size ($50M, $100M, etc.)
- Checks your project type (Acquisition, Divestiture, etc.)
- Applies industry standards

### 📊 Generates Production Data
- Creates 12 months of oil production (barrels/day)
- Creates 12 months of gas production (MCF/day)
- Adds water production data
- Applies realistic decline curves (15-20% annual)
- Adds price variations

### 💰 Generates Financial Data
- Creates monthly revenue based on production
- Calculates operating costs (OPEX)
- Adds capital expenditures (CAPEX)
- Includes taxes and royalties
- Applies inflation to costs

### 🧠 Smart Scaling
- **$50M deal** = 1,000 bbl/day oil, 5,000 MCF/day gas
- **$100M deal** = 2,000 bbl/day oil, 10,000 MCF/day gas
- **$25M deal** = 500 bbl/day oil, 2,500 MCF/day gas

### 📈 Realistic Patterns
- Production declines over time (like real wells)
- Costs increase with inflation
- Water cut increases over time
- Prices vary within market ranges
- CAPEX front-loaded (first month)
