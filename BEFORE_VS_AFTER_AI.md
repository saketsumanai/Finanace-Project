### 1. Strategic Alignment & Rationale: Why Are We Doing This?

**Reserve Verification:**
- P1 (Proven): What's the certainty level? (>90%)

**Infrastructure Assessment:**

### 3. Financial Analysis: The Numbers Game

**Valuation Methodology:**
- DCF (Discounted Cash Flow) - primary method
- Comparable transactions ($/boe, $/flowing barrel)
- Net Asset Value (NAV)
- Market multiples (EV/EBITDA, EV/Production)

**Key Metrics to Calculate:**
- NPV (Net Present Value) at 10%, 12%, 15% discount rates
- IRR (Internal Rate of Return) - target >15%
- Payback Period - ideally <5 years
- ROIC (Return on Invested Capital)

**Price Assumptions:**
- Oil: $70-85/bbl (base case), $60-100/bbl (sensitivity)
- Gas: $3-4/MMBtu (base case), $2.50-5/MMBtu (sensitivity)
- Escalation: 2-3% annually

### 4. Risk Assessment: What Could Go Wrong?

**Technical Risks:**
- Reserve uncertainty (±20-30%)
- Production decline faster than expected
- Equipment failures
- Well integrity issues

**Market Risks:**
- Commodity price volatility
- Demand fluctuations
- Competition
- Market access

**Regulatory Risks:**
- Environmental compliance ($X million/year)
- Permitting delays
- Tax changes
- Royalty adjustments

**Mitigation Strategies:**
- Price hedging (50-70% of production)
- Insurance coverage
- Contingency reserves (10-15% of capex)
- Diversification

### 5. Deal Structure: How to Structure the Transaction

**Payment Options:**
- All cash (simplest, fastest)
- Cash + stock (share risk/reward)
- Earnout (performance-based, 20-30% of price)
- Deferred payment (seller financing)

**Typical Structure:**
- 70-80% cash at closing
- 20-30% earnout over 2-3 years
- Working capital adjustment
- Escrow for indemnification (10-15%)

### 6. Synergies: Where's the Value Creation?

**Cost Synergies (Easier to Achieve):**
- Overhead reduction: $2-5M annually
- Operational efficiencies: 10-15% cost reduction
- Procurement savings: 5-10%
- Shared services: $1-3M annually

**Revenue Synergies (Harder to Achieve):**
- Production optimization: 5-10% increase
- Enhanced recovery: 2-5% reserves growth
- Market access improvements
- Technology application

**Timeline:**
- Year 1: 30-40% of synergies
- Year 2: 60-70% of synergies
- Year 3: 90-100% of synergies

✓ Well files and completion data
✓ Facility inspections
✓ Environmental assessments

**Financial:**

✓ Joint venture agreements
✓ Operating agreements
✓ Royalty obligations

**Legal:**
✓ Title opinions

✓ Regulatory compliance
✓ Litigation history
✓ Contracts and commitments

- New fields (<5 years): 8-12%

**Discount Rates:**

- High risk: 15-20%

**Forecast Period:**

- Possible reserves: 5-10 years

### 9. Negotiation Strategy: Getting the Deal Done

**Key Leverage Points:**

- Competing bidders
- Financing certainty

**Negotiation Tactics:**
- Start with data-driven valuation
